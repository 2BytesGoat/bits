## Ingredients

> [!abstract] Shopping List
> **Dough**
>
> - [ ] 🍺 1 tsp active dry yeast
> - [ ] 🍞 450 g 000 flour
> - [ ] 🫒 50 g olive oil
> - [ ] 🧂 10 g salt
> - [ ] 💧 240 ml warm water

^shopping-list

_One mix = ~750 g dough: 3 mid-size balls (~250 g each) or 2 huge pizza-oven balls (~375 g each)._

Depending on your mood you can also use:

- **2 huge pizzas**: split the same batch into 2 balls for a heartier meal
- **Lean version**: use instead 20 g olive oil and 270 ml of water - I'll be lighter but with the same dough feel

## Preparation

> [!question] Disclaimer
> I'm usually lazy and use my bread machine to make the dough. But you can totally do it by hand or with a stand mixer

### Step 1 - Mix

<!-- ![[dough-step-1.jpg]] ← image slots live under the step they show -->

1. Pour 240 ml warm water into a big wide bowl
2. Stir in 1 tsp active dry yeast, wait 5 min to check the yeast is alive
3. No foam after 5 min - start over with fresh yeast
4. Add 50 g olive oil, 10 g salt and all 450 g of the 000 flour
5. Bring together with a spatula - it comes together ragged and shaggy, that's normal

### Step 2 - Rest and knead

<!-- ![[dough-step-2.jpg]] -->

6. Leave the shaggy mass alone 30 min - the flour hydrates and the dough smooths itself out
7. Knead in the bowl 10 min - it should feel supple now, not sticky-chaotic

### Step 3 - Portion

<!-- ![[dough-step-3.jpg]] -->

8. Split the dough into 3 equal balls (~250 g each)
9. Coat the balls in a dash of olive oil and put each in its own container
10. Leave them on the counter for at least 30 min to rest

### (Optional) Step 4 - Cold rise

<!-- ![[dough-step-4.jpg]] -->

11. After the counter rest, throw them in the fridge for 12–48 hours - or up to a week, they only get better
