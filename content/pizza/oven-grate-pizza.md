## Ingredients

> [!abstract] Shopping List
>
> ![[all-purpose-pizza-dough#^shopping-list|flat]]
> **Sauce**
>
> - [ ] 🍅 350 g can ground tomatoes
> - [ ] (optional) dried oregano
> - [ ] (optional) dried basil
>
> **Cheese**
>
> - [ ] 🧀 450 g low-moisture mozzarella
> - [ ] 🧀 small handful of finely grated parmesan
>
> **Prepping**
>
> - [ ] semolina / cornmeal / regular flour
> - [ ] olive oil to make dough not stick

Depending on your mood you can also use:

- **Sheet pan instead of the grate**: works under the broiler, but it cooks slower and comes out toppings-on-a-cracker
- **No rolling pin**: you can also just stretch the dough by hand - though that's going to make more air pockets around the crust

## Preparation

### Step 0 - Prepare the pizza dough

1. Follow the recipe for [all-purpose pizza dough](dough/all-purpose-pizza-dough). If you're in a hurry you can stretch the dough by hand after leaving it to rest for 30 min

### Step 1 - Heat the broiler and the grate

<!-- ![[grate-step-1.jpg]] ← image slots live under the step they show -->

2. Set a clean oven grate on the second-highest rack position
3. Turn the broiler all the way up - that's the filament at the top of the oven
4. Give the grate a few minutes to heat, then press a wet towel on it - if it sizzles it's ready

### Step 2 - Roll the dough

<!-- ![[grate-step-2.jpg]] -->

5. Flour both sides of one ball straight from the fridge, unwarmed
6. Roll and turn with a rolling pin until even thickness throughout - evenness is what keeps it from burning in patches
7. Stop just shy of target thinness, oblong not round - it stretches more on the way in

### Step 3 - Drape and broil the bottom

<!-- ![[grate-step-3.jpg]] -->

8. Coat the top face of the dough in a thin film of olive oil - cold dough is stiff and easy to handle
9. Pull the grate out as far as it goes and drape the dough oil-side down
10. Pick up drooping edges and hang them over the nearest wire
11. Slide in, close the door, broil about 3 min until a few small burn spots show

### Step 4 - Dress and broil again

<!-- ![[grate-step-4.jpg]] -->

12. Pull the rack out and flip the dough with the creases up
13. Spoon a thin layer of sauce, less than feels right
14. Scatter the parmesan, then the mozzarella edge-first, working inward
15. Slide back in, door closed, broil 2–3 min
16. Pull when the cheese is as brown as you like, straight onto a cooling rack

### Step 5 - Rest, cut, repeat

<!-- ![[grate-step-5.jpg]] -->

17. Rest a few minutes to steam off, cut with a chef's knife
18. The grate holds its heat - dress the next pizza immediately
