## Ingredients

> [!abstract] Shopping List
> **Vegetables**
>
> - [ ] 🥕 2 carrots
> - [ ] 🧅 1 onion
> - [ ] 🧄 3 garlic cloves
> - [ ] 🌱 2 celery stalks
>
> **Meat**
>
> - [ ] 🍗 chicken backs + necks
>
> **Starch**
>
> - [ ] 🍝 140g egg noodles
>
> **Seasoning & Pantry**
>
> - [ ] 1 tbsp olive oil
> - [ ] 1 tsp dried thyme
> - [ ] 1/2 tsp dried rosemary
> - [ ] 1/2 tsp salt
> - [ ] 1/2 tsp black pepper
> - [ ] 1 bay leaf
> - [ ] 1 bunch fresh parsley
> - [ ] 1 bunch fresh dill
>
> **End**
>
> - [ ] 1/2 lemon
> - [ ] (optional) 180 ml heavy cream

Depending on your mood you can also use:

- **Boneless Chicken**: instead of using backs and necks you can go with thighs or breast and add 8 cups of chicken broth
- **Rice instead of noodles**: long-grain rice 180 ml goes in with the water (Step 2), LOW 5–6 h, loosen with boiling water if it over-thickens

## Preparation

### Step 1 - Saute vegetables

<!-- ![[soup-step-1.jpg]] ← image slots live under the step they show -->

1. Dice onion, carrots and celery
2. Mince the three cloves of garlic
3. Heat the olive oil in a pan over medium heat
4. For 5-7 min - saute the vegetables until softened
5. For 30-60 sec - add and stir the minced garlic until fragrant

### Step 2 - Prepare the pot

<!-- ![[soup-step-2.jpg]] ← image slots live under the step they show -->

6. Unwrap and wash the chicken meat
7. Place the chicken meat into the pot
8. Transfer the sauteed vegetables to the pot
9. Add the bay leaf, thyme, rosemary, salt and pepper
10. Cover with 1800 ml of water
11. Cook on LOW for 6-7 hours or HIGH for 3-3.5 hours

### Step 3 - Shred the chicken

<!-- ![[soup-step-3.jpg]] ← image slots live under the step they show -->

12. Chicken out, bones out — shred the meat with two forks
13. Return the shredded chicken to the pot

### Step 4 - Add the starches

<!-- ![[soup-step-4.jpg]] ← image slots live under the step they show -->

14. Add the noodles to the pot
15. Set on HIGH for 15-25 min

### Step 5 - Finish the soup

<!-- ![[soup-step-5.jpg]] ← image slots live under the step they show -->

16. Turn off the heat
17. Stir in the cream
18. Fish out the bay leaf
19. Cut the fresh parsley and dill, add them to the soup
20. Squeeze in the juice of 1/2 lemon
21. Taste for salt
